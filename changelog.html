Changelog

What each version of Docsly brought, newest first.

Version 1.5.2

November 25, 2026

Bug fixes and improvements.

Version 1.5.0

November 18, 2026

Send documents over Wi-Fi

Pass documents and whole folders to another device with Docsly on the same Wi-Fi network — straight from one to the other, encrypted, with no server in between.

  • Send over Wi-Fi — from a document, a folder or a selection
  • Receive over Wi-Fi — in the … menu
  • The same code on both screens shows it is the right device
  • Device not found? Connect with a QR code
  • No Wi-Fi around? One phone can share its hotspot

Also new

  • Share in the selection bar lets you choose how: over Wi-Fi, as a .docsly file or as a ZIP archive
  • A .docsly file is imported from any screen — no trip to Settings — then Folders or Files opens
  • On iPad, a document open beside its folder closes with a cross
  • A profile can now be deleted with its documents and folders — after one confirmation and your PIN or Face ID; copies in other profiles and the Inbox stay

Version 1.4.0

November 11, 2026

Docsly reads your documents now

Find a document by any word in it, copy numbers and dates in one tap, and get reminded before a document expires — all on this device.

  • Search by what's inside
  • Fields in one tap
  • Know when it expires
  • It stays on your device

Version 1.3.0

October 25, 2026

Share documents as a .docsly file

  • Open the “…” menu of a folder or a document — or select several items and share them together
  • With a password, only someone who knows it can open the file. Without one, anyone who has Docsly can

Receive .docsly files

  • Open a .docsly file from a messenger or from your files in Docsly — it asks for a password only if the file has one
  • Imported documents are added to yours; what you already have stays as it is

Restore all or just a part

  • Restore the whole backup, or pick profiles, folders and documents from it

Inbox: filed in one tap

  • Select documents, tap the move button, then tap a folder — they move right away, no extra step
  • Moved the wrong one? For a few seconds after a move there is an Undo — the document comes back to the Inbox

And also

  • “Select all” now takes folders and documents together
  • A locked folder always asks for its PIN
  • Bug fixes and stability improvements

Version 1.2.0

October 1, 2026

  • Create profiles for yourself, your family, or work and switch between them with a single tap
  • Choose a color to easily tell your profiles apart
  • Copy or move files and folders between profiles from the menu
  • Create folders within folders — for example, “Car” → “Insurance” → “Vehicle Inspection” → “Fines”
  • Easily move between levels, and see the full path when you choose a folder
  • Choose the app language separately from your phone’s language
  • Select profiles, folders, or single documents — only they go into the backup
  • Move and copy folders with everything inside them
  • Settings are grouped into clear sections
  • A ZIP archive warns you if a document could not be read
  • Bug fixes and stability improvements

Version 1.0.1

September 16, 2026

  • Document, folder and tag names and previews are now encrypted on your device, like the documents themselves
  • Shared files keep their extension, and previews load faster
  • Tag filters show each tag's color, plus stability fixes

Version 1.0.0

September 3, 2026

The first version of Docsly in the App Store.

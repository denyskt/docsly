Docsly in facts

Short facts about Docsly you can quote as they are, and the files to go with them. For anything else, write to info@getdocsly.app.

In one sentence

Docsly is a document vault for passports, contracts, insurance and receipts: documents are encrypted on the device with AES-256, and there is no account and no server.

In one paragraph

Docsly is a document vault app for iPhone, iPad and Android. It keeps passports, ID cards, driving licences, contracts, insurance, warranties and receipts in one place on the phone, organized in profiles, folders and tags. Document contents are encrypted on the device with AES-256; the key is protected by the iOS Keychain or Android KeyStore and stays on the device. There is no account and no server: documents are never uploaded, and documents and search work offline.

Facts

  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.0 or later; Android 10 or later.
  • Price: free to download from the App Store and Google Play.
  • Languages: the app speaks 18 languages.
  • Encryption: AES-256 on the device; the key stays on the device. How the encryption works.
  • No account, no server: documents are never uploaded. The app sends only anonymous usage and crash data — see the privacy policy.
  • Offline: documents, search and text recognition work without a connection.
  • Between phones: over Wi-Fi from Android to iPhone and back, with no server in between; or as a .docsly file, or through an encrypted backup.
  • On the App Store since September 2026.
  • Made by Igor Gasenko and Denys Kotenko.
  • Docsly is not open source.

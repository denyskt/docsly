Expiry reminders for passports and driving licences

Docsly reads the expiry date from the document itself and reminds you before it runs out — so a passport does not expire a week before a trip.

When the reminders come

30, 10 and 3 days before the document expires, on the day itself and the day after — at 10:00 local time. In the last month the document’s card also shows how many days are left.

Where the date comes from

Text recognition finds the date on the document. Docsly uses it only when it can trust it:

  • the machine-readable zone of a passport or ID card, when its check digit matches;
  • the barcode on the back of a US or Canadian driving licence;
  • field 4b of a European driving licence;
  • a printed label that reads the same on every page;
  • a bank card’s expiry next to a valid card number.

If a document holds several dates, the earliest one counts. The date cannot be typed in by hand, so text recognition needs to be on (Settings → Features → Text recognition).

Turning it on and off

Reminders are on by default: Settings → Features → Expiry reminders. On iPhone, iPad and Android 13 or later, Docsly asks for permission to send notifications the first time it finds a document that expires within 30 days. If you refuse, the setting stays on but no reminders arrive until you allow notifications in the phone’s settings.

Private by design

The notification never names the document. Tapping it opens the document after your PIN, Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint.

What it does not do

  • The schedule is fixed: you cannot pick other days or another time.
  • Documents in the hidden vault, and documents kept only in hidden folders, get no reminders.
  • A date the recognition could not confirm is not used, so a document without one of the sources above gets no reminder.

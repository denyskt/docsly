Scan paper documents and turn them into a PDF

Point the camera at a document: the edges are found and the page is straightened. Several pages become one document, and photos can be combined into a single PDF.

Scanning

Tap Scan document. Docsly opens the phone’s own document scanner: on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s document camera; on Android, Google’s document scanner. Both find the page edges and correct the perspective. Scan several pages in a row and they are saved as one document.

On Android one scan takes up to 20 pages. The Google scanner needs Google Play services; where it is not available, Docsly opens the ordinary camera instead.

Photos to PDF

  • Convert to PDF turns one photo document into a PDF.
  • Merge to PDF combines several selected documents into one PDF.

Before the PDF is made you can put the pages in order and give it a name. The PDF is saved as a new document; the photos it was made from stay where they were.

Other ways in

Documents can also come from Photos and Files, or from another app’s share menu (on iPhone and iPad it reads Save to Docsly). Photos, PDFs and videos live side by side in the same folder.

Kept encrypted

Scans are encrypted on the phone with AES-256 as soon as they are saved. To send one, share it like any other document — as it is, or as a .docsly file for another Docsly.

Forgot your PIN

Docsly has no account and no server, so there is no “reset PIN” link and nobody — including us — can unlock the app for you. What you can do depends on two things: whether Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock was on, and whether you have a backup.

If biometric unlock was on

Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint still opens Docsly without the PIN. Once inside:

  1. Make a fresh backup or Export to file right away (Settings → Data & storage → Backup & Restore), so your documents are safe whatever happens next.
  2. Changing the PIN asks for the current one, so a forgotten PIN cannot be changed from inside the app. Keep using biometrics, and keep that backup up to date.

Wrong attempts

The first 5 wrong PINs cost nothing. After the sixth the keypad locks for 30 seconds, and each further wrong PIN locks it for 1, 5, 15 and then 30 minutes. Nothing is ever erased by wrong attempts, so it is safe to keep trying the PINs you might have used. A successful Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock lifts the lock.

If there is no way in

Without the PIN and without biometrics, the documents on this phone cannot be opened. The only way to start over is to delete Docsly, which deletes its documents on this phone with it. Then install it again, set a new PIN and restore your backup or import your exported file. If, after reinstalling on iPhone or iPad, Docsly asks for the old PIN instead of a new one, write to info@getdocsly.app before doing anything else.

Without a backup, documents that existed only in Docsly on this phone cannot be recovered. This is the other side of the protection: a PIN that could be bypassed would protect nothing.

The second PIN of the hidden vault

A forgotten second PIN cannot be recovered either. From the main vault you can remove it — Settings → Security → Decoy PIN → Remove; this asks for your main PIN or biometrics.

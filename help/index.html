What Docsly does

  • Send documents between Android and iPhone over Wi-FiFrom Android to iPhone and back, over your own Wi-Fi, with no server in between.
  • Expiry reminders for passports and driving licencesA reminder before a passport, ID card or driving licence runs out.
  • Text recognition and search inside documentsFind a document by any word inside it; recognition runs on the phone, offline.
  • Scan paper documents and turn them into a PDFScan paper with the camera and combine the pages into one PDF.
  • Encrypted backups to your own Google Drive or iCloudAn encrypted copy of your vault in your own Google Drive, iCloud or a file.

Move your documents to a new phone

Docsly’s encryption key never leaves the phone it was made on. That is why moving a phone’s whole contents — an iCloud or Google device backup, a phone-to-phone setup — brings Docsly back empty. Move the documents with one of the three ways below, before you reset or give away the old phone.

Which way to choose

  • Same platform (Android to Android, iPhone to iPhone): an encrypted backup to Google Drive or iCloud.
  • Android to iPhone or iPhone to Android: a backup file, or Wi-Fi transfer.
  • Both phones in your hands, on one Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi transfer, folder by folder.

1. Backup to Google Drive or iCloud

  1. On the old phone: Settings → Data & storage → Backup & Restore → Create Backup. Choose a passphrase of at least 8 characters.
  2. On the new phone: install Docsly, sign in to the same Google account (Android) or iCloud (iPhone), open the same screen and restore the backup with that passphrase.

Google Drive is not visible from an iPhone and iCloud is not visible from Android, so this way works only within one platform. More: Restore a backup.

2. A backup file — works across platforms

  1. On the old phone: Settings → Data & storage → Backup & Restore → Export to file. Set a password and save the file where the new phone can reach it: a computer, a cloud drive, a message to yourself.
  2. On the new phone: open the file (tap it, or Backup & Restore → Import from file) and enter the password.

3. Wi-Fi transfer — works across platforms

With both phones on the same Wi-Fi, open Receive over Wi-Fi on the new one and Send over Wi-Fi on the old one. It sends the folders and documents you choose, not the whole vault in one go. How it works: Wi-Fi transfer.

After the move

  • The PIN is not carried over: the new phone uses the one you set when you installed Docsly on it.
  • Text recognition reads the documents again on the new phone; search by text fills up as it goes.
  • Check that your documents are there before you erase the old phone.

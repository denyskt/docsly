Your documents stay on your device

Docsly has no server for your documents. Document contents are encrypted with AES-256, and the encryption key never leaves your phone.

The app only sends anonymous analytics and crash reports. Document names and thumbnails are encrypted on the device too.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find an answer? Write to us — we’ll help you sort it out.

Write to us
Is Docsly free?

Docsly is free to download on Google Play and the App Store.

Does Docsly need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including text recognition, works offline. The internet is used only for backups you start and for anonymous analytics and crash reports.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no Docsly server. Documents leave your phone only when you share them or create an encrypted backup to your own cloud.

What happens if I lose my phone?

If you made an encrypted backup, restore it on a new phone with your passphrase. Without the passphrase nobody can open the backup — not even us.

Which languages does Docsly speak?

18, and the app language can differ from your phone’s.
